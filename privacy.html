Motion Alarm - Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026

This policy explains what the Android app Motion Alarm (package com.jaspercherry.motionalarm) does with your data. The app has no accounts, no servers of its own and no analytics. The only data that leaves your phone goes to Google, for ads and for the optional Remove ads purchase.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Settings such as sensitivity, timers and light pattern.
  • The alarm log: when you armed and disarmed the alarm, when it was triggered and how strong the movement was.

Both are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else. You can delete the log at any time with Clear on the Logs tab. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

Sensors, camera and screen brightness

  • Motion sensors (accelerometer and gyroscope) are read on the device to detect movement. Readings are not sent anywhere.
  • Camera permission is used only to switch the flashlight on and off when the alarm fires. The app never takes photos or records video.
  • Modify system settings is used only to dim the screen while the alarm is armed and brighten it when it fires.

The app does not use your location, contacts, microphone or files.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, general device information and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any ads are requested. You can change your choice later under Privacy settings at the bottom of the Manual tab. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings.

Remove ads purchase

Remove ads is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. The app never sees your payment details. It only asks Google Play whether your account owns the purchase, and keeps a copy of that answer on your phone so ads stay off when you are offline. Google's handling of purchases is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

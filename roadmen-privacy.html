Roadmen - Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026

This policy explains what the Android game Roadmen (package com.jaspercherry.roadmen) does with your data. The game has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics and no in-app purchases. The only data that leaves your phone goes to Google, for ads.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Game progress: the cities you have beaten and their stars, your units and their levels, your chicken wings and your chosen squad.

Your progress is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

The game does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Advertising

Roadmen shows rewarded ads from Google AdMob, and only when you choose to watch one for in-game wings. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, general device information and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before any ads are requested. You can change your choice later with Privacy options at the bottom of the main menu. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
